1Where things live

2Logging in

  1. Go to app.lawfirmmarketingpros.com and sign in with your own LFMP login.
  2. Use the account switcher at the top left to open [AGCY] Law Firm Marketing Pros. This is our own account. Client accounts have the client's name.
  3. Install the LeadConnector mobile app and sign in with the same login.
Tip: Before you change anything, check the name in the account switcher. It's easy to be in a client's account by mistake.

3Set up your account

Do these once, on your first day, while you're in the [AGCY] account. Bookings and emails depend on them.

  • Connect your calendar. Go to Calendars → Calendar settings → Connections, check that your own name is selected under My staff, click + Add new and connect your Google calendar. Under Configuration, choose your Linked calendar (where GHL bookings are added) and your Conflict calendars (busy times to block).
  • Update your email signature. Go to Settings → My Profile, find the Signature section, and add your name, title, LFMP phone number and the company logo. Turn on the option to include it in outgoing emails, then send yourself a test email to check it.
  • Check your profile. In Settings → My Profile, make sure your name, email, phone and photo are correct, so clients and teammates know who they're talking to.
  • Turn on notifications in the LeadConnector mobile app for new messages and anything assigned to you.
Tip: If your calendar isn't connected, people can book you over existing meetings, and your bookings won't show on your Google calendar.

4Conversations

Menu → Conversations
  1. Use the Unread and Assigned to me tabs to see what needs a reply.
  2. Open a conversation, pick SMS or Email at the bottom, and reply.
  3. If a conversation isn't yours, assign it to the right person from the top of the conversation.
Tip: Reply to clients and prospects from GHL, not from personal Gmail, so the whole team can see the history.

5Contacts, notes & tasks

Menu → Contacts
  1. Search by name, company, email or phone. Open the record to see conversations, appointments, forms, opportunities and notes.
  2. Add a note after every call or meeting: what was discussed and what happens next.
  3. Create a task with a due date for any follow-up, and assign it to whoever owns it.
  4. Check the Owner and Followers. Each record needs the right owner (see Team rules).
Tip: Search before you create a contact, so you don't make a duplicate.

601. Sales Pipeline

Menu → Opportunities → 01. Sales Pipeline

Every prospect has one card. Move it the same day something happens, so the pipeline shows where each deal really is.

#StageMove the card here when…
1New LeadsA new lead arrives. New contacts are added here automatically.
2Initial ContactWe've reached the lead for the first time.
3Right Fit CallA Right Fit Call is booked or done.
4Discovery CallA Discovery Call is booked or done.
5Strategy SessionA Strategy Session is booked or done.
6Price NegotiationThe proposal and pricing are being discussed.
7ContractThe contract has been sent.
  1. Set the owner and the opportunity value on every card, so forecasts are accurate.
  2. When the client signs, set the status to Won. This starts onboarding: the client tag, the welcome email and the intake form request.
  3. If the deal is lost, set the status to Lost and choose a reason, so we can learn from it.

702. OPS Onboarding Pipeline

Menu → Opportunities → 02. OPS Onboarding Pipeline

Tracks each new client from signed to fully onboarded. Several stages start automations, so keep cards in the right stage.

#StageWhat happens
1Status Changed to WonThe welcome email goes out, and Operations is reminded to collect the intake form.
2Intake Form SubmittedA Drive folder is created and Slack is notified.
3CSM Assigned & Welcome OrdersThe assigned CSM is notified and the welcome basket is ordered.
4Onboarding Form Submitted, Then 100 Day Kick OffThe onboarding form is in, and the 100-Day Kickoff is scheduled.
590-day Service Fulfillment AuditFulfilment review at the 90-day mark.
Tip: The Enhancement, PPC Only and DLF Only stages are for those service types. Ask Operations if you're not sure where a card belongs.

8Onboarding Hub

client-onboarding-iyf.pages.dev · sign in with your @lawfirmmarketingpros.com Google account
  1. Find the client: search their GHL contact by name, company, email or phone, then pick the form: Client Onboarding, AI Chatbot Questionnaire or LeadPro.Legal Onboarding.
  2. Fill it in with the client, or send a link: click Create client link, then copy it or email it. The client can fill it in and save without logging in, but can't submit.
  3. Review: client answers show under Client answers waiting for review and are marked From client. Reviewing locks the client's link (read-only). Unlock it if they need to make changes.
  4. Submit on the call: the last page has Submit. It saves a copy under Submissions, updates the GHL contact, adds the form's tag and closes the client link.
  5. The bell 🔔 tells you when a client starts or finishes a form, connects their calendar, or a teammate submits.
  6. LeadPro.Legal clients: in LeadPro.Legal account & calendar, set the account to Ready, pick their sub-account, tick who takes consultations, then mark each person Connected once you've checked in GHL.
Tip: Submitting the Client Onboarding form adds the tag that starts the onboarding workflow. Only submit it for real clients, never for tests.

9Team rules

  1. Owner = CSM, Followers = CSC/CSS on every client record. Change the owner by clicking the Owner name on the record.
  2. One record per person. Search before creating, and merge duplicates you find.
  3. Notes and history stay in GHL. Reply from GHL and log every call.
  4. Work stays in Teamwork. Don't use GHL tasks as a second project board for delivery work.
  5. No passwords in GHL. Keep them in Bitwarden and refer to the Bitwarden item.
  6. No test assets in live. Don't publish test workflows, forms or calendars, and don't create test contacts. Ask in #help-ghl for a safe place to test.
  7. Name things clearly. Use the existing numbering style, for example "3.0 - When…, Then…" for workflows. Never leave names like "copy" or "Form 35".

10By role

SalesOwn your cards in 01. Sales Pipeline: owner, value, a stage move after every call, then Won or Lost with a reason. Book calls in the GHL calendar so they land in the CRM.
CSMOwn your client records. Assign yourself as CSM to start the welcome steps, run the 100-Day Kickoff, and use the Onboarding Hub with clients. Keep client health on the Teamwork Stoplight board.
CSC / CSSFollow your CSM's clients. Track onboarding forms and access in the Onboarding Hub and chase missing client information. Your tasks stay in Teamwork.
OperationsKeep 02. OPS Onboarding moving: intake form, CSM assignment, onboarding form, 90-day audit. Check completeness and the handoff into Teamwork.

11Getting help

All GHL help goes through Slack, so nothing gets lost in direct messages.

GHL questions or accessPost in the #help-ghl Slack channel. Include a screenshot and the record, form or workflow name.
Need it fixed or set up? Create a ticketIn #help-ghl, tag @create-ghl-ticket in your message. This automatically creates a ticket and assigns it to the person responsible for GHL.
Tip: In your ticket, say what you clicked, what you expected and what happened. Don't try to fix live workflows yourself.
